# Questionnaire responses (classroom trial, 20 April 2026)

Anonymized responses from 33 third-year undergraduates who used the learning system
in one 180-minute class session. Timestamps, e-mail addresses, and the graded in-class
comprehension check (part of the course assessment, not used in the study) have been
removed. Row order has been randomized; the `respondent` column is an arbitrary index.

- `questionnaire_responses.csv`: one row per respondent. Q01-Q19 are five-point Likert
  items (responses kept in the original Japanese); the three `comment_*` columns are
  free-text answers in Japanese. Blank cells are unanswered items.
- `codebook.csv`: variable names, item groups, item wording in Japanese and English,
  the response scale, and whether the item is reported in the paper (Q16-Q19 on general
  AI literacy were collected but are outside the scope of the paper).

Respondents were informed that responses would be handled anonymously and used for
educational material development and research, and only students who consented answered.
